# PS: Wish You Were Here - A Virtual Photo Booth for Long-Distance Memories 💌

A labor of love, _PS: Wish You Were Here_ is more than just a photobooth—it’s a way to **bridge the distance** between people who can’t be together. Whether it’s best friends miles apart, long-distance couples, or family members in different time zones, this web-based photobooth brings them together in a single frame, as if they were side by side. 💕

## ✨ Features

### 📸 Capture & Arrange Memories

- Snap four photos in sequence
- **Auto-arrange feature** for effortless composition
- **Manual drag-and-drop** for personal touch

### 🎨 Customize Your Strip

#### Filters to Set the Mood

- Classic Black & White
- Warm Vintage Sepia
- High Contrast & Vibrant Tones
- Soft Focus for a dreamy effect
- Customizable filter combinations and many more

#### Choose Your Background

- 12 delicate pastel shades, from _Lavender Blush_ to _Powder Blue_
- Minimalist aesthetic to keep focus on the memories

### 💝 Make It Personal

- **Write a heartfelt message** (up to 15 words) that gets embedded in the strip
- _PS: Wish You Were Here_ signature feature ✨
- Elegant typography using **Playfair Display & Alice fonts**

### 🎭 Vintage Meets Modern

- Beautifully crafted double-bordered frames
- Subtle **hover animations** and transitions
- Delicate ornamental details (❦, ✿, ✧, ♡) for a timeless touch

## 💫 Why This Exists

I created this project because I believe in the power of **shared experiences**, even across screens. So many of us have someone we miss—someone we wish we could take silly photobooth pictures with. And now, we can.

This is just **one piece** of a bigger vision: a platform that lets long-distance friends, families, and partners do **more than just text and call**. I want to build **interactive, shared experiences**—this is just the beginning.
